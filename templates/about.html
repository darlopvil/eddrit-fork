eddrit is a lightweight alternative frontend for Reddit, inspired by old.reddit.com.

This instance runs darlopvil/eddrit-fork, a fork of corenting/eddrit with fixes and features that are not in upstream:

  • All media (images, GIFs, MP4 and DASH video) goes through the instance, so your browser never talks to Reddit's servers on its own.
  • Comment threads keep loading although Reddit blocks them for anonymous clients, through an optional logged-in session.
  • Comment permalinks and /s/ share links work instead of returning 404.
  • Images linked in posts and comments are shown inline.
  • Saved images are named after the post title.
  • A compact /embed view of a post, for other sites' iframes.
